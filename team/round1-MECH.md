# round1-MECH — gameplay engine (js/game.js). Read: PROTOCOL, CONTRACT, game.js, levels.js, sprites/sfx API, v1 shots.
## 1. CRITIQUE OF v1 (honest)
- Looks great, plays like a tech demo: every enemy just walks left, every upgrade is a linear dmg bump, nothing is ever in the way. No terrain, no Force, no mid-boss.
- Charge beam is a trap: builds free while autofiring (no trade-off), flies slower than normal shots (6.5 vs 8 px/f), and one pass does ~12 of the boss's 240 hp. Releasing = lifting the fire thumb = no fire. Nobody will use it.
- Touch: relative drag (gain 1.3) is right but un-felt: no ghost/feedback, no smoothing, zone split uses window width not the letterboxed canvas rect, pause/mute are 14px logical targets, fire zone invisible. Right thumb does everything; no way to add Force/bomb.
- Death snowball: dying drops shield+options, -1 power, instant respawn x=30 into the same wave; with 3 lives a bad moment ends the run. No rank/recovery. Loops only scale hp (+35%), never bullet speed/rate.
- Enemies: 7 behaviours in one switch, all enter from the right, all aimed-single-shot at 1.5 px/f. No ambush from behind, no ground/ceiling turrets, no formations that react, bullets never mix patterns. Stage 3 waves (12 butts @0.1s) clutter more than they threaten; ebul pool is 110.
- Boss: 3 attacks all from the same mouth; it tracks your y so it is a big slow target (the whole 96x80 body is vulnerable, the mouth is not a weak point). Swallow: only cue is jitter+roar, no lane marker, ignores shield (instant death), no counterplay. Phase intervals are the only difficulty knob. Attack ids are guessed by regex (atkId) and phases by 5 naming heuristics: ~150 lines of defensive code for a data format we now control.
- Hitboxes are good (8x6 player core, bullets inset 1-2px, 120f respawn i-frames fair; 70f after shield break). Keep.
- Score: kill-count combo (x1..x8, 2s) is flat; no risk/reward (no graze, no chain banking).
- Perf (500+ canvas calls/frame): sky = 224 fillRect with fresh rgb() strings; far layer = 60 one-pixel drawImage rows; 90 star fillRects; every shadow drawn twice; full-screen vignette (alpha blit) + scanline blit; S() does hasSpr/try/catch per draw; per-frame allocs (bossBodyRect, getWaves/Object.assign); render runs at 120Hz on 120Hz phones though sim is 60.
- Bug: render() calls rng() for shake, so the sim RNG is polluted by frame rate (blocks any deterministic replay). Needs a separate fx RNG.
- Structure: one 1700-line IIFE, string-typed state machines, Levels fallback `FB` duplicates levels.js. v2: behaviour/attack registries (`BEH[name](e)`, `ATK[id](b,params)`), trust Levels, delete FB and heuristics.
## 2. v2 PROPOSAL (priority order; P1 must ship, P3 only if time)
P1 Force pod: `pu_force` gives a pod (16x16). Attached front: blocks enemy bullets, 2 dmg/6f contact, adds forward shots by weapon level. Touch: TAP the left (move) thumb without dragging = throw/recall; key C. Thrown: flies right 7px/f, holds ~110px ahead, auto-fires, y lerps to ship. Recall: homes in and snaps to FRONT if it comes from the right, REAR (shoots backwards, great vs ambushers) if from the left. 2nd/3rd pu_force = pod lv2/lv3 (fire rate) -> replaces v1's weak orbiting options.
P1 Weapons: capsules `pu_spread` (5-way fan), `pu_laser` (short pierce beam, 3 dmg/tick, stops at terrain), `pu_wave` (sine bullets, pierce, hit ceiling+floor). P capsule = level 0..3 of current weapon; picking another type keeps level. Default gun stays.
P1 Charge rework: hold = autofire; after 0.35s the gauge fills, fire rate drops to 60% past tier 1 (the gun "wheezes" - the trade-off). Tiers at 0.5/1.0/1.6s: 8/16/30 dmg pierce beams (24x10, 40x16, 64x24), speed 9, plus tier-3 clears enemy bullets in its lane. Charge ring at the nose + haptic tick per tier. Keyboard X = hold to charge.
P1 Terrain: Levels gives per-stage `ceil[]`/`floor[]` height arrays (8px columns). Ship touching rock = hurt (shield absorbs, 1s i-frame, nudged out; no instakill - phone fairness). Player/enemy bullets die on rock with spark+ping. Floor shadows project onto the local floor height. Stage 1 flat, stage 2 gentle waves, stage 3 tunnels with 72px min gap. Turrets mounted on floor/ceiling scroll with terrain.
P1 Boss v2: mouth is the weak point (hp dmg x1 while open, x0.25 on hairy body) so it stops being a tank. Three clearly different attacks + filler: FAN (aimed fan with a gap that rotates), SPIRAL (2-arm hairball spiral 3s), LUNGE-SWALLOW (0.8s telegraph = red lane band over the lane it will cross + rumble). Shield + swallow = SPAT OUT (shield lost, 90f i-frames). No shield: 70f gulp where mashing fire (>=10 taps) = spat out as well, else dead. Comedic, fair, mobile-friendly.
P1 Mid-boss (`midboss` 48x40, ~45 hp, 2 attacks, drops a weapon capsule); stage clock freezes while alive, flees after 25s.
P1 Chain scoring: kills within 1.5s chain; mult x1..x8 shown as a bar under SCORE; graze (enemy bullet passes <7px of core) = +50 and +0.3s chain; popups coloured by mult; chain break banks "CHAIN 12 +2400" popup (cap 6 on screen). Replaces combo.
P1 Rank (hidden 0..1): +on chain/powerups, -on death. Scales enemy bullet speed/rate +-20%. Recovery: after a death with no power-ups a P and a shield capsule are guaranteed within 4s.
P2 Controls: thumb-stick ghost (ring at touch-down, dot at finger, 0.4s fade); ship follows target with critically-damped smoothing (feels analog, 2px deadzone); split taken from canvas rect (55/45); 24px logical pause/mute; small BOMB button (bottom-right, only appears when meter full) so panic-tap is possible. Haptics: navigator.vibrate on hurt 40ms, death 120ms, tier-up 8ms, bomb pattern, swallow telegraph 3x10ms; toggle in pause menu.
P2 Bomb "FART BLAST": meter fills from chain-multiplied score (full at 6000 pts). Tap = clears enemy bullets (they turn into score sparkles), 10 dmg to all on screen, 15 to boss, 60f i-frames, green fog flash + shake.
P2 New enemies (behaviour registry): `turret` (floor/ceil, aimed 3-burst), `worm` (8 segments on a sine path; only head is weak, segments are bullet sponges), `mine` (hairy drifter, splits into 4 shots), `ambush` (enters from LEFT edge, `from:'left'`). Plus carrier waves: last enemy flashes and always drops `wave.drop`.
P2 Raster: per-stage copper-bar sky from palette, stage 2 water reflection (flipped strip of the scene below y=waterline, per-scanline sine offset, 40% alpha, 2px rows), stage 3 horizontal copper bars additive (pre-rendered), phase-change palette flash. All pre-baked canvases.
P2 Perf: bake sky into 32 canvases (1 blit), merge scanlines+vignette into one overlay, shadows once with correct alpha, sprite-canvas cache (no hasSpr per draw), render only when a sim step ran, zero per-frame allocation, fx RNG separate. Budget <120 draw calls/frame.
P3 Instead of full replay: 3-frame hit-stop on big kills, 0.5x slow-mo for 40f on player death and boss kill. (Real replay = ring buffer of draw lists; I will do it only if everything above lands. I advise against: high cost, low phone value.)
Structure: I want to split into js/game.js (sim) + js/render.js (global `GameRender`: backdrop, raster, HUD, overlays). DIRECTOR: this changes load order (…levels.js, render.js, game.js). OK?
## 3. ASKS

- `Sprites.meta(name)` -> {hit:{x,y,w,h}, mouth:{x,y,w,h}, muzzle:{x,y}} (optional per sprite; boss mouth rect for frames 2-3 open, ship nose muzzle). Add `flipY` to Sprites.draw opts. Keep all v1 names/sizes.
- Force & weapons: `force` 16x16 6f (0-3 spin, 4-5 flash), `pu_force`, `pu_spread`, `pu_laser`, `pu_wave`, `pu_bomb` 12x12 2f each (keep `pu_option` as alias), `bullet_spread` 6x6 1f, `bullet_wave` 8x6 2f, `bullet_laser` 16x3 1f (I stretch it), `beam_t1` 24x10, `beam_t2` 40x16, `beam_t3` 64x24 (2f each, replace bullet_charged), `charge_orb` 16x16 4f (growing glow).
- Enemies: `turret` 16x16 4f (closed, open, firing, hit; base at bottom, I flipY for ceiling), `worm_head` 14x14 4f, `worm_seg` 12x12 2f, `mine_hair` 14x14 3f, `midboss` 48x40 4f (idle, idle2, mouth open, hurt).
- Terrain on an 8px grid: `ter_floor_top` 8x8, `ter_floor_fill` 8x8, `ter_ceil_bot` 8x8, `ter_ceil_fill` 8x8, each 4 variant frames; per-stage palettes as suffix sprites `bg_far_2/_3`, `bg_rocks_2/_3`, `bg_ceiling_2/_3`, `ter_*_2/_3`. Optional `boss_butt_2` recolour for stage 2/3.
- Procedurally by me (no art): stick ghost, bomb ring, water, copper bars, buttons.
SOUND
- Handles with `.stop()`: `charge` also needs `.setLevel(1..3)` (pitch steps) and `charge_tier` blip (opts.pitch ok); loop `laser` (handle).
- New names: `force_throw`, `force_recall`, `force_attach` (clunk), `shoot_spread`, `shoot_wave`, `bomb` (long wet blast ~1.2s), `bomb_ready`, `graze` (tiny tick, pitch up with chain via opts.pitch), `chain_up`, `chain_break`, `bullet_ping` (rock hit, very short), `turret_fire`, `midboss_warn`, `boss_phase`, `struggle` (gurgle tick per mash), `spit_out` (blech), `hitstop_thud`.
- Music: tracks `'midboss'` (reuse boss with lead muted is fine), `SFX.music.stinger('warning'|'clear'|'midboss_clear')`, `SFX.music.duck(ms)` for bomb/death, optional `SFX.music.setRate(0.5..1)` for slow-mo.
LEVELS
- Stage: `scroll` (world px/s), `terrain:{step:8, ceil:[px..], floor:[px..]}` (heights 0..56, multiples of 8; index = floor((stageT*scroll+screenX)/8); flat after end; min gap 72px; I will also call optional `Levels.terrainAt(stageIdx, worldX)->{ceil,floor}` if you prefer generators), `mounts:[{wx,type:'turret',side:'floor'|'ceil'}]`, `midboss:{t, type, hp, attacks, phases}`, `raster:['copper','water','heat']`, `waterline` (y), `spriteSuffix:'_2'`.
- Waves: `from:'right'|'left'|'top'|'bottom'` (default right), `drop:'pu_force'` carrier flag, new patterns `'stagger'`, `'ring'`. New types `turret`, `worm{segments}`, `mine`, `ambush`; per-type optional `hit:{x,y,w,h}` and `fire:{rate,type,spread,speed,aim,burst}`. Speeds ALWAYS px/s (I drop the >8 heuristic).
- Boss/midboss: `attacks:{fan:{n,gapRotate,speed,repeat,every}, spiral:{arms,dur,rate}, lunge:{tele,speed}, spawn:{type,n}, rain:{count}}` and `phases:[{hpAbove, seq:['fan','spiral'], interval}]`; unknown ids ignored. Stage 1 boss: fan+spawn, 2: +spiral, 3: +lunge. Stage 1 first 20s: butt_small only, <=5 on screen; first Force carrier by t=12, first weapon carrier by t=30.
- `Levels.powerupDrop(type, rng, ctx)` with ctx {power, weapon, hasForce, forceLv, lives, rank}; may return pu_force/spread/laser/wave/bomb; never return capsules the player maxed out.
