# ROUND 1 — SOUND (js/sfx.js)

## 1. Critique of v1 audio (synthesis + call sites)
**Synthesis is solid, the usage is thin.** All 23 CONTRACT sounds exist, voices are pooled (MAX_VOICES 16, oldest stolen), nothing throws. Problems:
- **Charge sound is out of sync with the game.** `startCharge` rises over 1.5s, but game.js starts it at 15 frames and the shot is armed at 36 frames (0.35s of audio, ~23% of the rise). The player never hears "ready". Needs a 0.35s rise plus a "ping" + held shimmer at full.
- **Pause/unpause restarts music at bar 0** (`musicStop()` then `music(curTrack)`); a hard cut, not a pause. Needs `SFX.music.pause()/resume()` (suspend the sequencer, duck bus, keep M.step).
- **Boss death is silent for ~8s.** `damageBoss` calls `musicStop()`, then 150 frames of dying, then 330 frames of clear with only a single `powerup` arpeggio as the "stage clear" fanfare. No victory sting, no between-stage music. Title music never returns after game over (`gameover` track ends, then silence until restart).
- **Boss dying fires `enemy_die` every 5 frames** on top of `boss_die` (2.3s). Combined with `enemy_die_big` noise, this will saturate the compressor (threshold -14, ratio 10) and pump the music, and steals SFX voices (16 cap) so shots and hits vanish.
- **Fatigue:** `shoot` (980→180Hz square zap) fires every 4+ frames, identical each time, ~15 shots/s autofire = ear-pain on mobile speakers. `hit` is a fixed 2kHz highpass tick, repeated per bullet. `fart` is randomized (good) but plays on every butt wave spawn AND every spit AND boss spread/spawn (6+ per stage in a row), so the joke dies by stage 2. Every enemy kind sounds the same on death (only small/big).
- **Mix:** SFX bus 1.0 vs music 0.35*; music is loud-ish (bass vol 0.34, kick 0.9) and `stage` (150bpm, 16 bars) is the same loop for all 3 stages. `levels.js` sets `music: 'stage'` on every stage. Nothing ducks the music, so big sounds fight the kick/bass in the 40-150Hz band (boss_die sine 60Hz, enemy_die_big 70Hz, kick 42-160Hz).
- **Dynamic music is barely used.** `setIntensity` only scales the lead gain: 0.3 at stage start, 0.8 at warn, 1.0 at boss. No hp-driven change, no boss phase layers, no stage progress ramp, no danger state. Intensity never reacts to combo or enemies on screen.
- **Init:** `firstGesture()` calls `SFX.init()` on pointerdown and keydown only (good, idempotent, and the title `music('title')` is queued via `wantTrack`). But it is NOT called on touchend/visibilitychange-return; iOS can re-suspend a context after the tab is hidden and `running()` then blocks every play with no resume attempt except the next press. Fix inside sfx.js: resume on `visibilitychange` and on any `play()` while suspended.
- **Missing cues:** low-hp warning, boss swallow telegraph (reuses `wave_warning`, which is also used for stage-end, boss intro, and 3 times), boss phase change (just `boss_roar` again), combo tiers, player respawn, stage intro, stage clear, hiscore, enemy fire telegraph (`butt_spitter` spit has no windup), powerup *appearing*, powerup variants all share `powerup`, game start jingle (reuses `menu_select`), menu navigation on the title (none).
- **No stereo.** Everything is mono-centered; a shmup with a 384px field benefits a lot from pan.

## 2. v2 proposal (all in sfx.js, one pass)
1. **Variation rules (inside `play`):** auto pitch jitter per sound (`shoot` +-6%, `hit` +-10%, `enemy_die` +-8%, `fart` already random); cap `shoot` voice to the 3 newest (quiet 0.7 gain when powerLv>=2 so 4-way spreads are not 4x loud). Accept `opts.variant` ('small'|'mid'|'spitter'|'charger'|'eye'|'squid'|'boss') on `enemy_die`/`hit` for distinct timbres: small = pop+squeak, mid = wet thud, spitter = splat, charger = rocket whoosh+bang, eye = glass pluck, squid = bloop sweep.
2. **Panning:** `opts.x` (0..384) -> StereoPanner, pan = clamp((x-192)/220, -0.8, 0.8). Cheap: one panner per voice, fallback to none if unsupported.
3. **Ducking:** a sidechain-style duck on `musicBus` (linear ramp to 0.45 for ~0.25s, recover over 0.5s) triggered internally by `enemy_die_big`, `boss_die`, `boss_roar`, `swallow`, `player_die`, `shield_break`. Also a per-sound priority table (boss/player > big die > pickup > shoot > hit) so voice stealing kills low-priority first, not oldest.
4. **New sounds:** `title_sting` (formant-synth "AAASSS MASTER!": sawtooth through 3 bandpass formants sweeping /a/ -> /e/, a hiss-noise "sss" tail, then a stab chord), `swallow_tele` (rising wet gurgle + sub-bass rumble, 0.8s, replaces wave_warning for the lunge), `boss_phase` (descending 3-note klaxon + roar), `stage_clear` (4s fanfare, drops music), `stage_intro` (short rising stinger), `respawn` (reverse-zap shimmer), `lowhp_beat` (sub-sine heartbeat lub-dub, 2 beats), `combo_up` tiers (x2 = 2-note blip, x4 = 3 notes, x8 = full arpeggio + sparkle; pitch rises with tier), `charge_ready` (ping + sustained shimmer), `spit_windup` (short gurgle ahead of a hair-ball), `pu_appear` (soft chime), `powerup` variants via `opts.kind` ('power'|'speed'|'shield'|'option'|'bonus'), `hiscore` (jingle), `menu_move` pitch step.
5. **Charge rework:** `charge` takes `{rise: 0.35}` (default 0.35 to match game.js); at the end it holds with an LFO shimmer and plays `charge_ready` once; `handle.level(0..1)` optional.
6. **Music:** keep the sequencer, add: (a) `stage1/stage2/stage3` tracks (Am-feel / Dm-minor-sus / phrygian-diminished, varied BPM 140-160, different arp+drums) and `stageclear`, `bossintro` (4-bar riser, no drums); (b) **layer system**: tracks define `layers` (hat, arp, lead, 2nd lead, drum-fill, bass-octave) with an intensity threshold each; `setIntensity` stays 0..1 but now fades layers in/out at the next bar (not just lead gain); (c) **boss phases**: `SFX.music.setPhase(n)` swaps in extra layers (phase 2 adds a fast arp + double-time hat, phase 3 adds a detuned lead and key up +2 semitones on the next loop); (d) **low-hp mode**: `SFX.music.setDanger(bool)` lowpasses the music bus to ~1.8kHz and adds a heartbeat, all fades 0.3s; (e) `pause()/resume()` (suspend the sequencer without losing the position, lowpass 600Hz while paused); (f) `music.start(name, {fade: s, restart:false})` crossfade instead of cuts; non-looping tracks fire an `onend` callback queue (`SFX.music.onEnd(fn)`).
7. **Robustness:** auto `ctx.resume()` on visibilitychange; `SFX.setVolume({master, music, sfx})` so the director/MECH can add a settings pop-up; `SFX.stopAll()` on stage transitions; keep `SFX._tracks` for tools.
